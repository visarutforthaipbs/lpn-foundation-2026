# LPN website positioning brief

Research date: 30 September 2026. This is a recommendation based on public sources, not a substitute for interviews with LPN staff, workers, donors, or partners. The site should serve people seeking help and supporters equally.

## Recommended position

**LPN is a frontline migrant-rights organisation in Thailand that helps people access protection today and builds safer systems for tomorrow.** It combines trusted, multilingual case support with education, worker voice, field research, and advocacy. Fishing rescues are an important proof of its courage and history, but they are one part of its work.

Short expression: **Rights within reach. Protection that lasts.** / **เคียงข้างแรงงานข้ามชาติ เพื่อสิทธิ ความปลอดภัย และโอกาส**.

Suggested homepage opening:

> LPN works alongside migrant workers and families in Thailand to access safety, healthcare, education, and fair treatment. When someone needs help, we respond. What we learn in communities helps us prevent harm and improve the systems people rely on.

Thai draft:

> LPN เคียงข้างแรงงานข้ามชาติและครอบครัวในประเทศไทย เพื่อให้เข้าถึงความปลอดภัย การรักษาพยาบาล การศึกษา และสิทธิที่ควรได้รับ เมื่อมีคนต้องการความช่วยเหลือ เราลงมือทำ และนำสิ่งที่เรียนรู้จากชุมชนไปผลักดันการเปลี่ยนแปลงระยะยาว

Two equally prominent paths should sit immediately below that promise: **Get help / ขอความช่วยเหลือ** and **Support the work / ร่วมสนับสนุน**. The help path should remain available in the header on every page, including donor pages.

## Why this position fits the evidence

- LPN's own [About](https://www.lpnfoundation.org/about) and [Services](https://www.lpnfoundation.org/services-1) pages describe three connected areas: rescue and victim assistance, migrant education, and rights advocacy. Its stated theory is that direct casework supplies evidence for prevention and advocacy, while community education builds trust and surfaces cases.
- LPN's [2024 annual report](https://d90624d9-477d-4d0e-8335-c4795fa13a13.usrfiles.com/ugd/d90624_78b68c9d8aff4a41856fc475cd1d5c37.pdf), published in May 2025, reports 135 worker-assistance cases benefiting 707 people, 832 workers trained, and 80 young people in an education and support project. These are dated annual results, not lifetime totals or evidence that every beneficiary received every service.
- LPN's [2023 account of its worker-voice programme with CPF](https://www.lpnfoundation.org/post/new-report-highlights-successes-of-lpn-and-cpf-worker-voice-and-worker-training-programme) describes a confidential complaint channel and worker training. This supports a business-partnership story, but the website should explain how worker confidentiality and LPN's independence are protected.
- A [September 2026 LPN report announcement](https://www.lpnfoundation.org/th/post/%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99-%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%93%E0%B8%A0%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%95%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%83%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%94%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%82%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%96%E0%B8%B6%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%93%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%82%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%A0%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%9E) says a new study gathered accounts from 100 fishers and family members in eight coastal provinces about health and access to care. It demonstrates a current evidence-building role beyond rescue.
- The [ILO's 2026 regional study](https://www.ilo.org/publications/towards-fair-seas-recruitment-and-working-conditions-migrant-workers) identifies continuing recruitment, wage, safety, and forced-labour risks in fishing and seafood processing. The wider [Thailand Migration Report 2024](https://www.ilo.org/publications/thailand-migration-report-2024) covers inclusion, rights, social protection, and healthcare. The issue is broader than trafficking alone.

## Position among adjacent organisations

This is an inference from each organisation's public description; these groups are potential collaborators as much as comparison points.

| Organisation | Public emphasis | Implication for LPN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Issara Institute](https://www.issarainstitute.org/) | Worker-voice systems, technology, recruitment, and supply-chain partnerships | Make LPN's direct case support, community trust, and education concrete; avoid claiming worker voice is unique to LPN. |
| [Human Rights and Development Foundation](https://hrdfoundation.org/?lang=en&page_id=2) | Migrant legal aid, test cases, labour-law clinics, and policy work | Show how LPN connects legal access with safety, shelter, healthcare, youth support, and community education. |
| [ITF Fishers' Rights Network](https://www.itfglobal.org/en/news/fishers-rights-network-calls-thai-government-enforce-contract-provisions) | Fisher organising and collective representation | Tell LPN's fishing story without implying it is the only fisher-rights organisation or speaking over worker-led groups. |

The credible distinction is the **connection** between individual help, community-led prevention, and evidence that can change institutions. Avoid unsupported exclusivity claims such as “only LPN” or “the first.”

| Visitor | Question the site must answer | First message | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Worker or family member | “Can someone help me safely, in my language, with this problem?” | LPN helps with work-related rights and access to support, including pay, documents, health, safety, and education. | Call or use a verified message channel. |
| Donor or institutional partner | “Is LPN effective, accountable, and relevant now?” | LPN combines direct help with prevention and evidence-backed change; here are dated results and current needs. | Read impact/report; fund or discuss a partnership. |
| Researcher, journalist, policymaker | “What does LPN know from the field?” | Research and case experience reveal where systems fail and what practical changes could help. | Explore reports and contact LPN. |

## What the website should tell and show

The homepage should answer, in this order: **Who is LPN for? What help is available? How does the work create lasting change? What happened recently? How can I act?**

1. **Hero:** broad promise and two clear actions. Use a real, consented photo showing people and agency rather than an anonymous distressed fisher or dramatic chains.
2. **Help panel:** visible phone and verified messaging channels; Thai, Burmese, Khmer, and Lao routes; plain choices for pay, documents, safety, health, and children. State response hours, confidentiality, and what happens after contact only when LPN confirms them. Do not require an online form for urgent help.
3. **Three work streams:** **Respond and protect** (casework, rescue, legal and health access), **Learn and strengthen communities** (education, youth, worker training), and **Change systems** (research, worker voice, advocacy, partnerships). Show one recent, sourced example in each.
4. **Dated impact:** a compact 2024 results panel linked to the annual report, distinguishing cases, people, trainees, and youth. Pair numbers with methodology and one consented human story. Include the 2026 fisher-health research as evidence of current work, not as an impact outcome.
5. **Supporter path:** a specific funding need, what unrestricted funding enables, recent reports, partner approach, and donation details. Present corporate partnerships transparently alongside worker safeguards.
6. **Publications and updates:** separate research/annual reports from ordinary news posts. Give reports year, language, topic, and a direct download.

Recommended top navigation: **Get Help · Our Work · Impact & Reports · About · News**, with **Support LPN** visible. Put Ghost Fleet and historical rescue stories under About/Stories, rather than making the film a main description of current work. The existing Projects page can become Partnerships, with past collaborations clearly dated.

On mobile, the help path should load quickly, use large tap targets, keep language selection obvious, and offer direct calls or verified messaging. For donors, use authentic field photography, clear typography, source-linked results, and restrained data displays. An interactive map is useful only if LPN can verify active service locations; an interactive outcome chart is useful only when comparable year-by-year data exist.

## Claims that need editorial control

- The current [Wix homepage](https://www.lpnfoundation.org/th) and [new Thai homepage](https://lpn-foundation-2026.vercel.app/th) lead with “modern slavery at sea” and a 2019 “7 in 10” statistic. The [ILO's later fishing analysis](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-12/thailand-migration-report-2024.pdf) uses a different forced-labour measure and sample. These figures should not be compared as a trend or used as an undated current headline. Move the older claim into a dated historical/research context after checking its original methodology.
- Confirm the period, source, and definition of the site's “4,986 fishers freed” lifetime figure before making it a hero proof point. Do the same for “15+ years,” daily hotline volume, and any rescue or shelter claims that may describe earlier operations.
- The current [donation page](https://lpn-foundation-2026.vercel.app/th/donate) frames every gift as fuel for rescue. That narrows the case for education, worker assistance, and advocacy. Replace implied one-gift/one-outcome claims unless actual unit costs are documented; confirm bank details before broader promotion.
- Do not publish identifying case details, exact safe-shelter locations, or unapproved images. Stories should show worker agency and consent, and separate a person's experience from generalised impact claims.

## Validation before a redesign is final

Ask LPN staff to verify current services, hotline capacity and languages, contact safety, geographic reach, 2025–26 outcomes, funding needs, and source approval for figures and stories. Test the help path with migrant-language readers and the support path with donors/partners. Track completed calls or messages and qualified partner inquiries without recording sensitive case details in analytics.
