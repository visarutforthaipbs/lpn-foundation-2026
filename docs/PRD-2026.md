# LPN Foundation website — product requirements

**Version:** 1.1 · 30 September 2026 · **Status:** proposed for LPN review

**Product owner:** LPN Foundation (named approver to be assigned)

**Scope:** the public website, its Payload CMS, and the connected LPN Right Guide journey; Thai and English on the main site at launch

**Strategic source:** [Positioning brief](./POSITIONING-STRATEGY.md) · **Current state:** [Migration status](./MIGRATION-STATUS.md), [Right Guide review](./RIGHT-GUIDE-REVIEW.md) · **Design constraints:** [Design guardrails](./DESIGN-GUARDRAILS.md)

## 1. Vision and product promise

LPN is a frontline migrant-rights organisation in Thailand that helps workers and families access protection today and builds safer systems for tomorrow. The website must make that relationship tangible: **individual help → community knowledge and prevention → evidence and institutional change**.

The public promise is **“Rights within reach. Protection that lasts.”** / **“เคียงข้างแรงงานข้ามชาติ เพื่อสิทธิ ความปลอดภัย และโอกาส”**. These are proposed editorial lines, subject to LPN approval and human Thai-language review. Every major page should answer at least one of two equally important visitor questions:

1. **Worker or family:** “Can I get relevant help safely, in a language I understand, and what happens next?”
2. **Donor or partner:** “What does LPN do now, what evidence supports it, and how can I contribute responsibly?”

Researchers, journalists, and policymakers are a secondary audience: they need findable, attributable field evidence and a route to the right LPN contact. Neither primary audience should have to pass through the other's story to reach its action.

### Problem to solve

The current public story overweights dramatic fishing rescues, older figures, and historical campaigns. It understates today's case assistance, education, worker voice, health access, research, and advocacy. Practical help is scattered among pages and languages; supporter evidence is mixed with news and undated claims. LPN also already runs [รู้สิทธิ ติดกระเป๋า / Right Guide](https://www.lpnrightguide.site/), a situation-based rights companion that the new site does not yet present as part of its worker journey. This makes the organisation harder to understand and the site less useful in urgent or due-diligence situations. The existing Next.js + Payload site has migrated the Wix editorial archive and blog; this PRD defines the product direction for **reorganising and connecting existing products**, not a fresh platform migration.

## 2. Product principles

| Principle | Requirement for content and design |
| --- | --- |
| Equal access to action | “Get help / ขอความช่วยเหลือ” and “Support the work / ร่วมสนับสนุน” are visible, distinct paths from the home page; help remains reachable from every page. They may have different visual treatments, but neither is buried. |
| Safety before conversion | Give a direct verified contact route and realistic expectations. Do not make a public form, account creation, or analytics consent a condition for urgent help. Never expose case details or shelter locations. |
| One LPN worker journey | The foundation site offers a clear choice between direct help and self-guided rights learning. Right Guide is the named learning tool, reachable without an account; it never gates the direct help path. |
| Agency, not spectacle | Represent workers as people with voices and choices. Use consented images and anonymised stories; avoid distress imagery as the default visual identity. |
| Claims with context | Outcome figures carry a period, definition, source, and review date. Historical accomplishments remain clearly dated. Research findings are not presented as LPN-delivered outcomes. |
| One connected theory of change | Each workstream and impact story shows how direct response, community capability, and system change reinforce each other, without implying every case creates policy change. |
| Local language is part of service | Thai and English pages are complete. Core help instructions and contact labels in Burmese, Khmer, and Lao require native-speaker and staff review before publication. Critical help copy must not silently fall back to another language. |
| Fast, accessible, maintainable | Prioritise low-bandwidth mobile use, accessible interaction, and CMS workflows that staff can keep current. An interactive element must clarify a decision or relationship better than a static alternative. |

## 3. Visitor journeys and success conditions

| Journey | Minimum successful visit | Failure to prevent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker/family facing pay, documents, safety, health, or a child's education problem | Choose direct contact or the Right Guide's relevant situation/topic; reach an LPN-verified phone or message channel at any point; understand service availability and the next step when confirmed by LPN. | Stale number, unsupported language promise, unsafe form, learning tool that hides urgent help, or a claim of instant response LPN cannot deliver. |
| Individual donor | Understand LPN's current work and dated results; distinguish unrestricted and specific needs; reach verified payment details or a verified donation provider. | Suggesting a fixed gift guarantees a rescue or presenting old numbers as current. |
| Institutional partner | See approach, evidence, safeguards for worker confidentiality and independence, past versus current partnerships, and a named inquiry route. | Treating a corporate logo as proof of an active partnership or hiding conflicts and worker safeguards. |
| Researcher/journalist | Locate a report by year/topic/language and see the method, publication date, source, and contact. | Mixing a report with a blog opinion or stripping dates/attribution from a statistic. |

## 4. Information architecture and page contract

The canonical navigation is **Get Help · Our Work · Impact & Reports · About · News**, plus a visible **Support LPN** action and language selector. On mobile, Get Help and Support LPN remain visible or one tap away without opening multiple layers. The final labels should be tested in Thai and English.

| Destination | Purpose and required content | Existing content treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home `/[locale]` | Clear promise; two paths; direct help strip; three connected workstreams; one dated result set; one worker-led story; current report; support route. | Replace the sea-only lead and undated hero statistics; retain fishing history in context. |
| Get Help `/[locale]/get-help` | Two unmistakable options: **contact LPN now** and **know your rights with Right Guide**. Add plain issue choices, verified contact methods, supported languages, hours/expectations if confirmed, safety/privacy note, and external emergency options only if verified. | Consolidate contact and services help copy; introduce the existing guide as a first-class tool while keeping direct phone/message access. |
| Right Guide (existing focused app; preferred future canonical `guide.lpnfoundation.org`) | Search, eight situation paths, 12 rights topics, Thai/English/Burmese learning, text sizing, and direct help exit. Relevant main-site pages link to stable guide topics. | Keep the separate PWA deployment for the first release; align brand, contact data, privacy and review controls. Preserve `lpnrightguide.site` links if the domain changes. |
| Our Work `/[locale]/our-work` | Three workstreams with current examples, geography only when verified, eligibility/limits where useful, related stories and reports. | Consolidate `/services` and relevant `/projects` content; preserve meaningful deep links with redirects or page-level continuity. |
| Impact & Reports `/[locale]/impact` | Dated results, definitions, methods, annual reports, research, filter by year/topic/language, accessible downloads. | Move substantive publications out of undifferentiated News; use the 2024 annual report as an initial sourced set. |
| Stories `/[locale]/stories` | Consented, anonymised stories showing people’s choices, LPN’s role, and what can and cannot be concluded. | Include Ghost Fleet and historic rescue accounts as clearly dated history, not the current organisational summary. |
| About `/[locale]/about` | Mission, values, origin/history, leadership, governance, transparency, team and contact. | Integrate existing About and Team; keep team detail accessible. |
| News `/[locale]/blog` | Current posts with date, author/source, topic and language; distinguish announcements, press, and analysis. | Retain all 83 published migrated Wix posts and six unpublished drafts in Payload. Preserve old article URLs. |
| Support `/[locale]/donate` | Current funding needs, unrestricted support, verified payment instructions/provider, donation FAQ and partner inquiry; link to Impact & Reports. | Replace rescue-only framing; verify bank details before promotion. |
| Contact `/[locale]/contact` | Office and media/partnership contact, with Get Help clearly separated for confidential case matters. | Retain useful existing details, then verify them. |

No migrated article or important page copy is discarded to simplify navigation. Redirect superseded routes, keep archival source material in Payload, and test old Wix and new-site links after cutover.

### Home page composition

1. **Hero:** “who LPN serves + what changes” in one short statement, a real consented image, and the two primary paths. No floating text on a busy image.
2. **Help strip:** issue examples, a verified direct channel, and a distinct “Know your rights” route to Right Guide immediately visible on mobile; no alarming imagery or speculative service promise.
3. **Three workstreams:** Respond and protect; Learn and strengthen communities; Change systems. One current example and a relevant next link per stream.
4. **How change happens:** a compact, accessible case-to-system diagram or stepper. Its plain-text equivalent must carry the same meaning. Show feedback loops without claiming automatic policy wins.
5. **Evidence:** a dated result panel linked to the report and metric definitions, paired with a consented story. A current research item shows ongoing work separately from outcomes.
6. **Support and latest updates:** the funding/partnership path, one specific current need when approved, and recent reports/news.

This order serves both primary audiences near the top, then explains LPN’s distinctive contribution. The home page should pass a five-second comprehension test: a new visitor can state whom LPN serves, what LPN does, and where to go next.

### CMS implementation intent

Keep **Next.js + Payload + Postgres + Vercel Blob** and the local Payload development database. Extend existing `pages`, `posts`, `media`, and globals rather than duplicating migrated records. Reports, metrics/claims, stories, and contact channels may be new collections or reusable structured blocks; choose the simpler model that enforces review metadata and lets editors update the site safely. Store language variants and visible “last reviewed” dates for high-risk help and donation content. The guide currently reads committed CSVs and remains separately deployed for P0; establish one approved contact registry or automated cross-product check before linking it prominently. An immutable source URL/file and editorial approval record should accompany each prominent statistic and legal-rights topic. Do not store confidential case intake in public analytics or the editorial CMS.

## 6. Visual and interaction direction

The brand should feel **steady, humane, and competent**: dark/white high-contrast foundation, restrained yellow for attention and action, strong typography, aligned content edges, generous spacing, and real human photography used with consent. Use the existing [design guardrails](./DESIGN-GUARDRAILS.md) as the base. Where its general advice to avoid multiple competing CTAs conflicts with the two equal-priority journeys, make the two choices a deliberate pair with explicit labels and no third competing action.

| Design choice | Vision served | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two immediately visible, differently labelled routes | Both core audiences know the site is for them. | Five-second and first-click tests with workers and donors. |
| Calm, high-contrast help pages with large tap targets and concise wording | A visitor under stress can act without decoding a campaign narrative. | Mobile usability with migrant-language readers; keyboard/screen-reader checks. |
| Clearly separated “Contact LPN” and “Know your rights” choices | Urgent help is immediate; independent learning remains available and dignified. | Users identify the correct path on first click and can move from any guide topic to direct help. |
| A shared LPN identity with a simpler, brighter guide interface | The two apps feel connected while the guide stays easy to scan as a practical tool. | Cross-site testing finds the same organisation, clear back/help routes, and no loss of guide readability or persistent contact access. |
| Consented, contextual field photography showing agency | Trust and dignity without rescue spectacle. | Every image has rights/consent review and meaningful alt text or is marked decorative. |
| Source-linked results with date and definition beside the number | Supporters can assess credibility without hunting through reports. | A new reader can explain what each headline number counts and when. |
| A simple case-to-system relationship graphic with text equivalent | Makes LPN's differentiated work understandable. | Users can explain the three streams and their connection after viewing. |
| Restrained motion and progressive disclosure | Keeps low-bandwidth and high-stress paths fast and readable. | Reduced-motion preference respected; core information visible without animation or interaction. |

Do not add a live map, animated counter, carousel, or donation calculator for visual novelty. Each requires verified data or a demonstrated usability gain. Data display should state uncertainty and scope; it should never imply causal impact from a single story. Support Thai text expansion and mobile widths from 320 px upward. Target [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) across complete pages. For real-user performance, target the [“good” Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals/) at the 75th percentile on mobile and desktop: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1. Use lab testing before launch and field data once traffic permits.

## 7. Editorial and evidence rules

- Lead with useful facts, plain language, worker agency, and specific LPN actions. Avoid “saving voiceless victims,” unverified superlatives, and a sea-only identity.
- Distinguish **activity** (cases handled, workers trained), **reach** (people benefiting), **outcomes** (a documented change for a person or institution), and **research** (evidence gathered). Do not combine them into a single impact total.
- The 2019 “7 in 10” fishing claim belongs only in a dated, sourced historic/research context after its original method is checked. Do not compare it with a later ILO measure as if they form a trend. Verify the period and definition of the “4,986 fishers freed” lifetime claim before prominent use.
- Each metric and report needs an owner, source, period, definition, approval date, and next review date. Time-sensitive help/contact and payment details need a shorter staff-defined review cycle.
- A story needs documented consent for its specific channels and image use, a safety check, anonymisation where needed, and a removal/correction path. Never publish exact safe-shelter locations.
- Thai and English editorial copy should be reviewed as writing in each language, not treated as machine-equivalent strings. Staff/native-speaker review is mandatory for migrant-language help copy.
- Right Guide topics are rights information with potentially immediate consequences. LPN assigns a reviewer for each Thai, English, and Burmese version; show the source/effective context and last-reviewed date, and provide a fast correction or withdrawal path. Do not advertise Khmer or Lao guide content before those full versions exist.

## 8. Measurement and learning

These are **product measures**, not invented baseline targets. Establish baseline after launch and review by language/device monthly; set numerical improvement targets after the first measurement period.

| Outcome | Measure | Interpretation guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Help is reachable | Share of tested worker tasks that locate the correct channel; contact-link starts by language/device; failed-link reports. | A click is intent, not a completed conversation or case. LPN may count real assistance separately in its secure operational system. |
| Rights learning is useful | Worker first-click success between direct help and Right Guide; guide scenario/topic task completion and failed-search reports, measured without identifying the visitor. | Topic views and search activity are learning signals, not proof that a legal problem was resolved. |
| Supporters understand and act | Task-test comprehension of the three streams; report views/downloads; donation starts and verified completions if the provider exposes them; qualified partner inquiries. | Do not equate visits, clicks, or downloads with donations or partnerships. |
| Evidence is trustworthy | Share of prominent claims with source/period/definition; overdue contact/claim reviews; correction requests. | Release goal: 100% of prominent claims and public help/payment channels approved and current. |
| Content is findable | Successful old-URL redirects; organic landing pages; internal search/filter success if search is added. | Preserve the migrated Wix archive even when navigation changes. |
| Site works for everyone | WCAG 2.2 AA audit findings; mobile task completion; Core Web Vitals by locale/device. | Audit real flows and content, not only automated scores. |

Analytics must not record case descriptions, sensitive query parameters, personal identifiers, stable per-person RFID hashes, or form field values. Audit the existing guide's Google Analytics and RFID URL handling before cross-site instrumentation. Obtain any required consent and document retention before deployment of analytics or third-party embeds.

## 9. Release plan and decision gates

1. **Content and operations verification:** LPN confirms current services, safe channels/numbers (including Burmese `0963812069` on the new site and `084-121-1609` on Right Guide), languages, hours/response expectations, geographic scope, bank/provider details, stories/images, partnership safeguards, and approved 2025–26 evidence. Review Right Guide's 12 topics and translations for legal accuracy, source dates, and safety. Missing information stays unpublished or is explicitly framed as unknown.
2. **P0 implementation in existing stack:** build the navigation and page contracts, structured CMS fields, bilingual content, redirects, and privacy-conscious instrumentation. Make Right Guide the named “Know your rights” branch while preserving its focused PWA deployment. Preserve the Payload migration and localhost development copy described in [Migration status](./MIGRATION-STATUS.md).
3. **Usability and editorial QA:** test both primary journeys with actual representatives, including migrant-language readers; check low-end mobile, keyboard/screen reader, translations, claim/source links, payment/contact links, historical URL inventory, guide deep links/offline claims, and cross-site contact consistency.
4. **Public-domain cutover:** only after P0 acceptance, LPN content approval, accessibility/performance checks, and rollback/redirect plan. The Vercel preview is a review environment until then.
5. **P1 discovery:** add visualization, geography, and expanded languages only when the data, safety review, and maintenance owner make them useful.

### Launch acceptance checklist

- [ ] A worker can reach a verified help channel in Thai and English from every page; migrant-language help routes shown publicly have been reviewed and tested.
- [ ] Home and Get Help offer direct contact and Right Guide as distinct routes; relevant guide topics link back to direct help, and no unsupported guide language is advertised.
- [ ] Right Guide's public numbers, 12 topics and translations, legal sources, metadata, privacy/RFID analytics, and claimed offline behavior have passed the checks in [Right Guide review](./RIGHT-GUIDE-REVIEW.md).
- [ ] A donor can explain current LPN work and find source-backed, dated results and a verified way to give.
- [ ] Every prominent number has a source, period, unit/definition, and approval; no historical figure appears as an undated current result.
- [ ] No confidential case data, unsafe location, unconsented story/photo, or visitor-entered sensitive data appears in public content/analytics.
- [ ] Thai and English primary journeys are complete; no critical help copy silently falls back to another locale.
- [ ] Migrated posts/pages and old URL inventory pass redirect/content checks in local and preview environments.
- [ ] WCAG 2.2 AA review and mobile task tests have no unresolved blocker on help, donation, reports, or navigation.
- [ ] Contact/payment routes have been tested end to end by LPN; the site has a named editor and review schedule.

## 10. Decisions LPN must supply

These are content/operational decisions, not reasons to stop building reusable structure:

| Decision | Why it matters | Default until confirmed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact available help channels, hours, language coverage, and what happens after contact | Prevents unsafe or misleading service promises. | Publish only verified channel details; omit unconfirmed hours and response guarantees. |
| Right Guide topic reviewer, correction owner, contact-number mapping, and canonical domain | Makes an existing LPN product safe and discoverable as part of the new website. | Keep its separate app on `lpnrightguide.site`; hold prominent topic deep links until content/contact review; use `guide.lpnfoundation.org` only after DNS and redirect checks. |
| Current services, geographic reach, and referral boundaries | Determines help categories and service-map eligibility. | Describe only confirmed scope; no map. |
| 2025–26 results and source approval | Enables a genuinely current impact story. | Use clearly dated 2024 annual-report figures and current research, separately labelled. |
| Donation account/provider, gift restrictions, and immediate funding needs | Gives supporters accurate action and accountability. | Hold unverified payment promotion; present a verified inquiry route. |
| Story/image consent and corporate-partnership review policy | Protects workers and institutional independence. | Use approved existing material only; no new identifiable case story or active-partner claim. |
| Editorial owners and review cadence | Keeps help and claims accurate after launch. | Mark time-sensitive records as pending and block publication until an owner is assigned. |

**Out of scope for this release:** confidential case-management/intake software, automated legal advice, a codebase merger of Right Guide and the main site, claims of guaranteed response or impact, live maps without verified safe data, and a full four-language site without translation ownership. These may be separate products or later releases after discovery.
